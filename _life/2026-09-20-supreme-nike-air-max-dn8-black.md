My latest Supreme pickup just landed: the **Supreme x Nike Air Max DN8 in black**, plus a Banner Mini that came with the order.

## The Shoe

![Supreme x Nike Air Max DN8 in black with co-branded box](/assets/images/life/2026-09-20-supreme-air-max-dn8-black.jpg)

The DN8 is the silhouette that doubled down on the original Air Max Dn's Dynamic Air — **eight pressurized tubes** running the full length of the foot (four in the heel, four in the forefoot), versus the Dn's four-tube heel unit. It's a lifestyle shoe first, but the cushioning has a genuinely fun, springy feel underfoot.

What sold me on the Supreme version:

- **All-black everything.** Black mesh upper, black midsole, black Air tubes. Goes with literally everything in the rotation.
- **The oversized outlined "SUP" on the lateral panel.** Supreme's collabs with Nike work best when the branding is loud but the colorway stays wearable — this one nails that balance.
- **The box.** Matte black Nike box with the red Supreme bogo sticker on the side. Small detail, but the co-branded packaging is half the fun of a Supreme Nike drop.

## The Banner Mini

![Supreme Banner Mini with gold tassels](/assets/images/life/2026-09-20-supreme-banner-mini.jpg)

A mini replica of the Supreme store banner — red satin with the white logo and gold tassel fringe. It ships in a plastic sleeve and is honestly one of those Supreme accessories that has zero practical purpose and I bought anyway. It'll hang somewhere in the office.

## Why This Pair

I've been wearing the DN8 silhouette since it launched and the comfort-to-bulk ratio is right for daily wear — lighter than it looks, and the full-length Air makes long days on concrete noticeably easier. The Supreme take keeps the stealthy black colorway while adding just enough branding to make it a *thing*. Into the rotation it goes.
