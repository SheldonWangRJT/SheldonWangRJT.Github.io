Just sold one from the archive: the **Supreme x The North Face Faux Fur Nuptse Jacket in Green** — new with tags, size L, gone on alias for **$1,137** (net **$991.01** after fees).

## The Piece

![Supreme x The North Face Faux Fur Nuptse Jacket in green, on-body editorial](/assets/images/life/2026-09-27-supreme-faux-fur-nuptse/on-body.jpg)
*On-body editorial — photo via Supreme / Hypebeast*

This was **FW20 Week 16** (dropped December 10, 2020): Supreme and The North Face took the classic Nuptse silhouette and wrapped it in shaggy faux fur — hunter green, black, and cherry red — over 700-fill down with a Windwall lining. Retail was **$578**. Supreme and TNF patches sit on the right sleeve, with the TNF logo embroidered on the chest and back and a "700" on the left sleeve.

![Supreme x The North Face Faux Fur Nuptse Jacket in green, official product shot](/assets/images/life/2026-09-27-supreme-faux-fur-nuptse/product.jpg)
*Official product shot — photo via Supreme / Hypebeast*

## The Math

Nearly six years after the drop, the green colorway still clears roughly **2x retail** in new condition. Asking levels on the resale market hover around $1,060, so $1,137 was a strong exit — the net $991.01 lands about **1.7x the original $578 retail**. Not bad for a jacket that spent most of its life in a box.

Honest footnote: this was a hold-to-sell piece from day one. Condition stayed new, it never got worn, and the market did its thing. Some pieces you keep forever; this one did its job in the archive and moved on.
