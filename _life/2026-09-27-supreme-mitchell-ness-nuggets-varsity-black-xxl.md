## Why this piece

The **rainbow skyline** is one of the most beloved logos in NBA history — the 80s/90s Denver Nuggets visual identity, rainbow stripes running into a mountain skyline. Mitchell & Ness went all in on it here: full rainbow appliqué across the chest, chenille "Nuggets" in green and red, green stars and purple satin stripes down the sleeves straight off the old warm-up jackets, the Mitchell & Ness script on the cuff, and the NBA logo on the right sleeve. Black wool body, snap-button front, striped ribbing at the collar, cuffs and hem. Hardwood Classics patch plus the Supreme box tag at the hem. It's loud in all the right ways.
