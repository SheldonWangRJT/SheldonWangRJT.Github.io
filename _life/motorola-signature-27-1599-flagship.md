## What Motorola announced on November 5

Motorola announced full specifications, pricing and availability for the Signature 27 on November 5, 2026. The phone was first teased on September 22 at Qualcomm's Snapdragon Summit, but the November announcement is the one that matters: this is the first time Motorola has put a price on its most ambitious phone ever.

In the US it costs $1,599.99 for the 12GB RAM and 512GB storage model. Pre-orders open November 19, with sales starting November 26 through Motorola's own website, Amazon and Best Buy. It reaches Europe, the Middle East and Africa on November 16, and Motorola has confirmed an India launch later this month, with the local price still unannounced. Gadgets360 lists the starting price at EUR 1,599 in Europe. Motorola also launched the Moto Watch 2 Fit and the Moto Buds 2 Ultra alongside the phone.

## The spec sheet

On paper, nothing is missing. The Signature 27 runs Qualcomm's Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6, built on a 2nm process, with prime cores clocked up to 5.01GHz and an Adreno 850 GPU. The 6.78-inch Extreme AMOLED Duo display is a tandem OLED with a 120Hz LTPO refresh rate and a peak brightness rating of 12,000 nits, which Motorola calls the brightest display available in a smartphone — a manufacturer claim, and a lab peak figure rather than a sustained brightness number.

The cameras are led by a 50MP Sony LYTIA 910 main sensor and a 200MP periscope telephoto with 3x optical zoom, stretching to 100x with Motorola's AI-based Super Zoom Pro, plus a 50MP ultrawide and a dedicated action sensor — a first for a smartphone. The phone has received a DXOMARK camera score of 173, the second-highest in the tester's global ranking, according to Malaysian Wireless. The battery is 7,000mAh with 90W wired and 50W wireless charging, the phone ships with Android 17 and seven years of OS updates, and it is Motorola's first device with official GrapheneOS support. Audio is tuned by Bang & Olufsen.

## The price problem

The hardware is not the risk. The price is. At $1,599, the Signature 27 costs more than the iPhone 17 Pro Max ($1,199) and the Galaxy S25 Ultra ($1,299) — Motorola is asking buyers to pay a premium over Apple and Samsung for their best phones, not under them.

That is a strange position for this brand. Motorola built its modern reputation on value: the Moto G line and the Edge series won buyers by giving them most of a flagship for much less than a flagship. The Signature 27 reverses the formula. It is Motorola's most expensive phone ever, and it is a bet that the brand can sell prestige, not just specifications.

The two numbers doing the heavy lifting are both the kind that need independent verification. The 12,000-nit display is a peak lab figure, and the DXOMARK 173 is a test score — neither is a review of the phone in your hand. The first real reviews will matter more than either number, and US buyers cannot get the phone until November 26.

## The judgment

On paper, this is the most complete Android flagship of the year. The question is not whether the hardware is good — it is whether anyone will pay $1,599 for it from Motorola. The phone's real test starts November 19, when pre-orders open: if it sells, Motorola has a new top tier; if it does not, the Signature line becomes a halo product that exists to make the rest of the lineup look reasonable.

Either way, two decisions in this phone are the ones other makers will be asked about next: a 12,000-nit display that makes every other screen look dim on a spec sheet, and official GrapheneOS support on a mainstream flagship. The price is the gamble. Those two are the ideas.
