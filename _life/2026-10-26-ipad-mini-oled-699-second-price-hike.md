## What Apple actually announced

On **October 26, 2026**, Apple unveiled what it calls the iPad mini's biggest update ever, and for once the superlative is mostly earned:

- **8.5-inch Super Retina XDR OLED** (up from an 8.3-inch LCD), 1,200 nits peak brightness — more than double the previous model — 600 nits full-screen, 2,000,000:1 contrast, 1-nit minimum, in the same footprint thanks to bezels 24% thinner.
- **A20 Pro**, built on a 2-nanometre process, with a 7-core GPU carrying Neural Accelerators in every core. Apple claims up to **4.5× faster AI performance**, up to **2.7× graphics**, and up to **60% faster multithreaded CPU** work versus the A17 Pro mini.
- **5.4mm thin** (down from 6.3mm) and, per Apple's specs, about 255g for the Wi-Fi model (down from 293g).
- **IP67 water and dust resistance** — a first for any iPad — rated for 1 metre of water for 30 minutes.
- A **12MP Center Stage front camera moved to the landscape edge**, an "Acoustic Display" speaker system built into the sealed body, Apple's **N1** chip (Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6, Thread), and the **C2 modem** on cellular models, claimed up to 50% faster than before.
- Pre-orders open **Wednesday, October 28**; availability **Friday, November 6**, in 36 countries and regions.

That is a real generational jump: screen, chip, radios, cameras, durability, and industrial design all moved at once. The argument is not about the hardware. It is about the price — and about what the spec sheet hides.

## The price: $499 → $599 → $699 in two years

MacRumors put the number in its headline, correctly: this is the mini's **second price hike this year**.

| iPad mini starting price (Wi-Fi, base storage) | Price |
| --- | --- |
| 2024 model at launch (128GB, doubled from 64GB at no charge) | $499 |
| Same model after Apple's mid-2026 increase (memory/chip shortages) | $599 |
| New OLED model, October 26, 2026 (128GB) | **$699** |

That is **+$200, or +40%, in two years — for the same 128GB of storage**. The cellular model rose in lockstep, $749 → **$849**. Education pricing starts at $649. The full Wi-Fi ladder is $699 (128GB) / $799 (256GB) / $999 (512GB), and the new TechWoven Smart Folio is **$79**, up from $59 for the old folio.

One mitigation, honestly noted: the rumoured increase earlier this month was $200 over the then-current model. Apple charged $100. In the year of chipflation — the same memory squeeze that, as we report today, is forcing Samsung and Apple to cut phone component orders — a $100 step for a first OLED panel, a new chassis, and a sealed IP67 body is not extortionate on its own. What makes it sting is the stacking: the June hike bought existing buyers nothing at all, and this one arrives on top of it. The mini's identity was "the small, relatively affordable iPad." At $699 it now starts $50 below where the 11-inch iPad Pro once lived, and the word "mini" describes only its size.

## The part of the spec sheet Apple didn't announce on stage

Apple's own tech-spec page, as MacRumors flagged within the hour, reveals that **"A20 Pro" is two different chips**:

- **128GB and 256GB models:** 5-core CPU (2 performance + 3 efficiency cores) and a single 16-core Neural Engine.
- **512GB model ($999):** 6-core CPU (2 + 4) **and a Dual 16-core Neural Engine** — 32 neural cores — plus more than double the unified memory bandwidth, per Apple's press release.

All three share the same 7-core GPU. Separately, the **nano-texture glass option exists only on the 256GB and 512GB models**.

Chip binning tied to storage tiers is not new for Apple, but it deserves to be said plainly: the customer who pays $699 for the headline product does not get the headline chip. The performance gap will likely be invisible in reading and browsing — the mini's actual jobs — and very visible in exactly the on-device AI workloads Apple spent its press release promoting (local LLMs, Draw Things, Detail are Apple's own examples). If you are buying this mini *for* on-device AI, the honest entry price is not $699. It is $999.

The range narrowed elsewhere too: four finishes became **two** — Midnight and Starlight. And Touch ID remains in the top button; there is still no Face ID, on a $699 tablet, in 2026.

## The verdict

Buy the screen, not the storage. The OLED panel, the 5.4mm sealed body, and the landscape camera fix every long-standing mini complaint in one generation, and pilots, clinicians, field workers, and one-handed readers — Apple's own named users — will feel the IP67 rating and 1-nit floor more than any benchmark. For them, $699 is defensible.

For everyone else, the math has changed. The previous A17 Pro mini is already being discounted below its $599 list (HotHardware noted $559 at Amazon today), and it still does everything a small tablet was for. The new model's premium is real but narrow — unless your workload is on-device AI, in which case Apple has quietly moved the real product to $999 and the $699 model is the decoy.

That tiering, more than the $100, is the story of this launch: in a shortage year, even Apple's smallest tablet now sorts its buyers by how much silicon they can afford.
